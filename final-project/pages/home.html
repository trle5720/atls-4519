Girls With Books!

Empowering South Sudanese Girls Through Education

About the Organization

Girls With Books! was formed through the merger of two long-running nonprofit organizations focused on education in Sudan and South Sudan. This partnership allows the organization to expand its reach and impact across multiple regions.

Programs now operate in Bor, Juba, and refugee camps in northern Uganda, supporting girls and young women who have been affected by conflict and displacement.

Our Mission

The mission of Girls With Books! is to educate South Sudanese girls and young women to become peacebuilders and positive change-makers in their communities. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Why This Work Matters

Many girls in South Sudan face barriers to education due to conflict, poverty, and displacement. By providing access to schooling and leadership opportunities, Girls With Books! helps create long-term change and stability.

Leadership

The organization is led by Executive Director Micklina Kenyi, who brings lived experience as a refugee and a strong background in education and advocacy.

Girls With Books! continues to rely on community support and donations to expand its programs and reach more students.
