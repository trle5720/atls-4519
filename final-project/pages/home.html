Introducing...Girls With Books!

We are pleased to announce that Empowerment Through Education Foundation (ETE) and Project Education South Sudan (PESS) have joined together to operate as one organization, known as Girls With Books!, effective October 1, 2022.

The merger of two small but effective organizations allows us to help more South Sudanese girls and young women obtain an education. We combine the board member experience of two organizations who have each operated for 17 years in Sudan, and after independence, South Sudan. Since we have operated in different geographic areas of the country, we will now expand our reach. We will work in three locations: Bor, the capital of Jonglei state, Juba, the national capital of South Sudan, and in the refugee camps of northern Uganda, where over 800,000 South Sudanese have fled to escape unrest in their own country.

The merger will allow us to streamline and unify programs under the leadership of Executive Director Micklina Kenyi, who came to the US at the age of 24 as a war refugee in 2004, became a US citizen in 2008 and received her master’s degree from CU in Educational Foundation Policy and Practice in 2014. Daniel Gai - who helped found PESS and who has served in a leadership role for many years - will also continue working with the organization from Bor, South Sudan, where he lives with his family. A well-established network created by both ETE and PESS will continue to assist the efforts of Girls With Books!, both in the Denver area and in South Sudan and Uganda.

The mission of Girls With Books! is to educate South Sudanese girls and young women to become peacebuilders and positive change-makers in South Sudan. We will focus on educating girls from many of the 64 ethnic groups in the country, bringing them together to help build peace where there has often been conflict. The combined effect of hundreds of girls graduating from school and taking their place as leaders in their community will have a powerful effect on the future of this young nation.

We thank all of you who have contributed generous amounts of both time and financial support to ETE and PESS over the past 17 years. We honor your service and your gifts and we ask for your continued support as we continue this great effort under the name Girls With Books! We are excited to share this happy news with you and we will reach out with additional information soon.
