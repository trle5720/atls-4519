Empowering South Sudanese Girls Through Education

Empowerment Through Education Foundation (ETE) and Project Education South Sudan (PESS) will join together and operate as one organization, known as Girls With Books!

Empowerment Through Education Foundation

The Empowerment Through Education Foundation works closely with schools, families and refugee camps to send girls to safe boarding schools where they can thrive.

Project Education South Sudan

Project Education South Sudan has been finding ways to fulfill our mission in the fast-changing environment of South Sudan.

Girls With Books merges two 17-year-old organizations that expands educational support for South Sudanese girls and young women across Bor, Juba, and refugee camps in northern Uganda. We hope to broaden our geographic reach to serve the 800,000+ South Sudanese who have fled to Uganda.

Led by Executive Director Micklina Kenyi, a former war refugee turned US citizen, earned her master's in Educational Foundation Policy and Practice from CU. Along side Daniel Gai, a co-founder who will continue leading efforts on the ground in Bor, they will streamline the organizations' existing networks in Denver, South Sudan, and Uganda will support the newly unified Girls With Books!

Girls With Books! aims to educate girls from across South Sudan's 64 ethnic groups, uniting them as peacebuilders and community leaders. By bringing together girls from communities that have historically been in conflict, the organization believes that hundreds of educated female graduates will create a powerful, lasting impact on the future of the young nation.

We are deeply grateful to everyone who has contributed their time and financial support to ETE and PESS over the past 17 years. We honor your dedication and generosity, and we invite you to continue supporting us as we move forward together under our new name, Girls With Books! We are thrilled to share this exciting news and look forward to reaching out with more details soon.
